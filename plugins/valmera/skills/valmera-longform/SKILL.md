## Engines and proof

- `story.py` finds ideas on the web, researches exact source quotes and verifies them against fetched pages. A long film is a story, not an explainer: each idea names its protagonist, the one dramatic question the film answers last and what is lost if they fail, and its `strength` rates the story, never the footage; among ideas with enough real footage the story decides (footage only breaks a near tie). The writer (and the archive conform after it) follows `STORY_SHAPE`: a cold open on a moment, the protagonist, the stakes and the question inside twenty seconds, a promise that poses the question and never gives away the answer or method, rising stakes, an ending at the payoff, each fact said plainly once (no 'this was not X' corrections, no 'remember' callbacks). It writes for the ear (`EAR`, and `ear.py`'s long-form shape): thoughts run on with commas and joining words, a full stop only where a thought lands (about one every fourteen words), lengths mixed widely, because Phonon reads only the words and punctuation and every full stop is a falling end. One or two focus words, ordinary joins of 0.18–0.26 seconds, and intelligible spoken forms of difficult words. A pronunciation change must preserve meaning.
- Before the final read, `archive_catalog.py` inspects the actual archive frames. `archive_script.py` conforms the researched draft to those views, checks its claims independently against verified quotes, and anchors visual subject changes to spoken words. Original drawings and ground rehearsals receive truthful context labels. The prior draft and read remain recoverable until delivery; the final encoded-picture gate still checks every shot.
- Once the spoken wording is audited and frozen, `archive_visual.py` checks a provisional picture edit alongside the guarded read. It can repair picture choices and labels, but cannot change the recording's words or chapter turns. It preserves checked intervals with word anchors when replacing a paragraph's other choices. Unresolved alternatives become research notes for the recorded word clock; they cannot erase existing source candidates. Its estimated coverage is planning evidence; only the final rendered film establishes the targets. Full-resolution still details and exact extracted report pages stay attached to technical figures, so a small thumbnail or missing caption context cannot decide their identity.
- `cinema.py` uses the shared Easton reader (`../valmera-stories/voice.py`, built on `../_shell/narrate/narrate.py`): whole sentences, expressive takes selected by signals, focus emphasis, tight joins. `longform_voice.py` measures the result and paces a hurried read against the Madigan references and adjusts its joins, then hears it again. It never resynthesises pitch: the contour expansion it used to apply (gain 1.24 on the 16 Nov read) deepened every sentence-final fall and gave the voice a processed sheen. Keep the owner's free Easton voice. Its pinned additional dependency is in `requirements.txt` for the narrator environment.
- `footage_box.py` calls `worker/footage` per chapter, with each line and its named subject. Discovery probes real video, not photo counts. The established Mac YouTube wall is handled on the box's existing fetch network; whole-source downloads support local cuts when remote sections fail. `witness_evidence.py` verifies original-voice attribution from source text and checks on-camera presentation, without identifying or comparing faces. No service restart or production switch change. Each original source interval is reserved for this film, including earlier chapters. Never fill a gap with a repeated image, frozen frame or unrelated footage. Cut real voices in where the subject speaks and level them to the narrator.
- Fetch jobs use an isolated container pinned to an installed worker image, so production rollouts cannot remove their scratch. It inherits only the existing fetch route, has no database credentials, records costs locally. With `CODEX_PROXY_LOCAL=1`, a private loopback SSH tunnel routes box calls to the Mac subscription adapter; the copied network guard blocks paid hosts, and the tunnel and scratch are removed on exit. Subscription calls are single-attempt, with one picture review in flight. If repeat video searches leave a rare event unfilmed, use a unique, checked archival evidence photograph with movement; photographs can never displace the majority-moving film gate. Search primary archive APIs and cited article captions as well as Commons. Alternate scans and mirrors reserve the same photograph. When genuine technical diagrams are needed, `document_evidence.py` researches original licensed reports, selects and visually verifies their figures, and shows them as archive records; it never synthesizes historical hardware. Its worker dependency is pinned in `requirements-worker.txt`.
- `music_chapters.py` uses MUSIC2's chooser, arrangement and mix for each *story chapter*, following its own turn and payoff. Retain the licensed track credits. SND3 designs recorded effects against the voice and music. Never synthesize effects.
- `chapter_audio.py` feeds MUSIC2 the levelled narration stem, checks SND3 against the actual chapter bed, ducks that bed for earned hero hits, and masters the lossless components together once. Retain the actual post-duck stems; an encoded voice-and-effects submix is not a narration stem.
- `moving_picture.py` offers unused moving alternatives to checked stills. It accepts a replacement only after the entire spoken interval is covered and independently checked; a failed replacement preserves the original edit. Source-review reuse binds the exact words, context, labels, provenance, original bytes and checker version. Every encoded picture is checked again after rendering.
- Continuity repair can extend either a neighbouring picture or a brief archive insert across the combined words, only after that complete pairing passes review. Source limits, uniqueness and the hold/movement requirements still apply; it never keeps a one-frame insert merely to add a cut.
- `encoded_repair.py` retains encoded mismatches and duplicate-picture verdicts, pins passing intervals, and repairs failed pairings on the existing spoken clock. An encoded rejection overrides a cached source preview; a new label cannot restore the same failed frames under those words. Two repaired films are the automatic limit, persisted across resumes. Every repaired film passes the complete final gates again. The reviewer receives verified context for the required end-card transition; its picture samples and historical-match criteria remain unchanged.
- Exact-gap research uses the footage engine's supported caller-verification mode. Its output is explicitly marked as research candidates: WF4's context/provenance check on the spoken interval replaces the generic subject-only filter, and the encoded-picture gate remains mandatory. Candidate counts are never reported as verified film coverage.
- A model transport failure or a scan with no checked frames is incomplete research, never evidence that footage is unavailable. It stops resumably and cannot count toward the two completed searches before a photograph fallback.
- A shared check that finds join defects triggers bounded, smooth level rides through `join_repair.py`, preserving all samples and timing. The unprompted VO2 measurement and the complete shared gate both run again; neither gate is relaxed.
- Completed witness splices retain a fingerprint of their inputs and audio/word outputs. A resume continues picture repair on that combined clock, with each interview slice carrying its actual full quote as context. It still passes both source and encoded-picture checks.
- A still is never a small print on a flat page: a landscape photograph (3:2 or wider, at least 960 px) fills the frame with a push-in; a portrait or machinery still stays whole as a large card over a blurred, darkened fill of itself (`cinema.still_fill`), with the card's own move.
- The scene renderer supplies picture; the existing brand finish supplies the unchanged watermark and end card. Heavy Mac work goes through `heavy.sh`; narration uses the shared kernel lock. `guarded.py` records `/usr/bin/time -l` and physical footprint, and stops its own process group if a process exceeds 6 GB. The same guard covers verification listeners.

## Delivery gates

When completed footage and figure research leaves an unfilmed action, `primary_passage.py` can find a short passage in an original witness transcript or official report. It first consults the primary passages already retained with the archive, preserving their attribution and reusing their original PDFs, before searching the web. It verifies literal source text and crops the actual page, with visible attribution; it never retypes a quotation or presents testimony as event photography. These passages remain still images, must be readable, unique and relevant to their exact words, and pass the unchanged source and encoded-picture checks.

Before delivery, measure the finished file and retained evidence:

- 600–1200 seconds; hook before ten seconds; a factual turn per chapter.
- At least 50% of picture time real moving footage. No repeated original source range or image. No hold over eight seconds without movement. `measure_picture.py` uses judge3’s unchanged cut and camera detectors on the encoded film; an invisible split in the edit plan cannot pass the hold gate.
- Check every displayed picture against its spoken words, including identity, era and context. Keep the checked frames and verdicts. Every encoded interval supplies its three temporal samples plus a full-resolution midpoint frame. Published Commons and Flickr archive captions retain their source URL, original description and HTML hash; requested labels are never treated as provenance.
- Music present under at least 80%; at least one measured rise per chapter. `measure_sound.py` retains the final-minus-clean diagnostic and uses the independent WF1-r4 rater’s final stem method for the verdict: fit the captured voice/SFX/music stems to the encoded film, find SFX onsets blind, and apply its two audibility tests (6 dB over the two-second non-vocal median, then within 6 dB of concurrent voice plus music). A poor stem fit fails. Never report planned events as audible events. Music downloads stay inside the run.
- Unprompted narration listener word error below 1%, including a second listen to the final encoded mix with music and effects present. Original-voice inserts are excluded from that narration comparison. The aligned voice stem locates silence for ASR window boundaries; every mixed sample still reaches the listener, with no script prompt. A music bed must not force artificial mid-word cuts at 25 seconds. Prior listener results and the exact windows remain in the evidence. Match the VO2 independent method on the retained narrator stem: pauses of at least 0.15 seconds, 11.7–17.4/minute, median 0.22–0.28 seconds; voiced p95–p5 pitch range inside the long-form narrators' band (`ref_band.json` 04: 7.39–14.62 semitones; the metromedia reels' 13.79–15.33 is reached by performance, never by resynthesis). Preserve rejected takes and diagnostics until fixed; never label an unmeasured target passed.

Failed quality gates are repaired and then recorded on the delivered film, never a stop (17 Nov, owner's rule of 16 Nov): the narration gate after its join repairs (`gate-carried.json`), the word error after the read's retakes, moving footage under 55% after two research passes (`moving-short.json`, archive pictures fill the rest), the still share (`still-share.json`), and the delivered-file targets (`measured.json` `defects`, after up to three encoded picture repairs). A crash or a stopped process still stops resumably. For persistent spoken-word errors, stop that run’s narrator and use `read_repair.py <run-folder> <diagnosis.json>` with `{"lines":[{"i":zero_based_segment,"evidence":"listener mismatch"}]}`. It rewrites against verified quotes, requires a second factual check, preserves unchanged takes and archives the changed ones. The thirty-take generation budget persists across resumes; the final word gate is unchanged. Diagnose and repair the product, then resume the same run; never hand-edit a customer's project. A completed run delivers once and removes its work folder. Keep film, source/credit ledger and measurement evidence. Run `judge3.py` for signal measurements and make a sixty-second comparison with the best matching long-form reference. A separate Claude Opus-or-above session owns the rubric-v3 quality rating; the builder does not rate itself or claim ALIEN from density alone.
