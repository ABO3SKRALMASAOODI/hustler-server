# Coordinator QC v7

## Acceptance order

Review in this order. A later strength never cancels an earlier blocker.

1. **Identity:** correct run, child project, EDL version, preview, and checksum.
2. **Story:** self-contained meaning and payoff in the assigned structure;
   a hook-to-montage short can resolve visually after its complete spoken premise.
3. **Opening/ending:** no clipped first word; no dead, frozen, or accidental
   tail; ending lands deliberately.
4. **Taste:** assigned style lane is recognizable and the result feels
   intentional rather than templated.
5. **Visual continuity:** reframing, speaker changes, B-roll relevance, asset
   cleanliness, overlays, transitions, and safe areas.
6. **Captions:** transcript fidelity, readable semantic grouping, timing,
   spelling, line breaks, and collision avoidance.
7. **Technical:** playable video/audio streams, assigned output ratio and
   measured dimensions, allowed duration, no accidental black/frozen corruption,
   and no unresolved render job.

When the named-style brief applies, enforce `style-lanes-v7.md` independently:

Review each assignment's `style_choice_reason` and `closest_alternative`
against the material, not just whether the edit imitates its assigned lane.
Before dispatch and after each wave, review the distribution and the reasons
for any dominant lane. A 15–16-to-one-each pattern demands a fresh content/asset
assessment; it is not justified by the old Style 4 default. No equal-share
quota applies. Record concrete reasons for keeping or changing assignments.
The lane rules below cover the editorial portion, followed by the required
native Valmera ending for the current brief.

- `fast-conversation`: frequent, fast, relevant B-roll with coherent continuing
  dialogue. Sparse or uniformly slow cutaways fail the requested treatment;
  2–3-second video holds are valid when their action earns them. No individual
  video B-roll insert may exceed 5 seconds in this brief; this is
  not a maximum hold for the main conversation.
- `hook-to-silent-montage`: a complete approximately 5–10-second spoken hook,
  then a montage until the editorial ending. The montage must be no more than
  15 seconds and the complete editorial program must be no more than 25 seconds;
  both exclude the separate native Valmera ending. Inspect the ENTIRE post-hook
  editorial interval for
  speech, inserted-clip audio, dialogue captions, or a return to the podcast
  scene. Any of those is a blocker even if the edit is otherwise polished.
  Compare every montage shot with its relevance ledger and the actual pixels.
  The viewer should immediately recognize the person, the specific work,
  or a direct visual consequence of the spoken premise. Generic adjacent
  subjects and multi-hop associations fail even if factually connected in a
  research note; e.g. anonymous astronauts do not automatically communicate
  Elon Musk or his personal arc. Check that archival/current material is not
  misrepresented as footage of another time or event. When a personal
  struggle-to-success premise is used, the montage should build that visible
  contrast with attributable material, not unrelated spectacle.
- `silent-action-to-conversation`: approximately 3–4 seconds of recognizable
  video of the actual subject doing something, without visible speech or
  audible clip sound, then conversation fulfilling the opening headline.
  A still image, unrelated person, or muted talking-head clip fails the opener.
- `headline-conversation`: no B-roll, external opener, or montage; only the
  coherent podcast scene and natural camera/speaker cuts. The same truthful
  topic headline remains visible above the picture for the entire editorial
  portion, including pauses, joins, its first frame, and its last frame. Accurate
  changing speech subtitles remain readable separately, horizontally centered
  around the middle of the visible picture rectangle as in the references.
  Verify their placement against the inner picture bounds across camera cuts;
  small central-area adjustments to protect faces are allowed. Subtitles in
  the lower black margin or a default bottom-caption position fail this lane's
  layout. An opener-only headline, missing subtitles due to text suppression,
  or any B-roll is also a blocker for this lane.

Inspect the assigned composition in the rendered pixels. For the current
default, check the black 9:16 canvas, wider straight-edged picture panel, and
headline area; a tightly cropped full-height portrait does not match. For an
explicit rounded-card variant, check its curved corners, background, margins,
and readable inset text. Changing the ratio alone proves neither composition.

For the current brief, inspect all headlines at phone size: they must use
bold/heavy lettering, visibly at least as thick as the dialogue captions and
read as the dominant title. A thin headline above heavy captions is a blocker,
even when the requested font setting claims to be bold.
Also inspect every headline area and sourced lower third for a small secondary
date/place/interview-location line. Reject incidental metadata decoration.
When chronology or location is essential to the story, it belongs in the
primary headline or intelligible dialogue, not an automatic subheadline.

Inspect the entire B-roll ledger and every rendered shot boundary. Within one
short, reject a repeated still, repeated source-time window, or near-identical
visual moment, including repetitions disguised by crop, zoom, mirroring,
speed, grade, or text. Distinct moments from one longer source are acceptable.
Across different shorts, repetition is allowed but should be avoided when an
equally relevant fresh shot exists; cross-short reuse alone is not a blocker.

Preserve and check both Valmera brand elements using `style-lanes-v7.md`:
the complete native ending and a legible corner watermark using the appropriate
admin choice: Current corner, Lower corner, or Scene top. Pass suitable native
placement that is visible, safely placed, and clear of faces and text. Do not
fail it for differing from an earlier illustrative side or exact coordinate.
Repair ordinary placement issues through the admin controls; keep other work
moving. Missing, cropped, covered, shortened, duplicated, or unreadable branding
still needs correction. Required branding is not
third-party contamination or an accidental tail. Reserve its actual runtime
within the final limit. Final-only branding is pending until the downloaded
final is checked; a preview pass alone never proves this part of delivery.

## Evidence schedule

Use the same current preview for every check. Inspect:

- 0.00, 0.25, 0.50, 1.00, and 2.00 seconds;
- every shot, crop, overlay, B-roll, caption-style, and transition boundary,
  with frames immediately before and after;
- each story-beat boundary and the payoff;
- every two seconds through otherwise uncovered intervals;
- the last two seconds at 0.25-second spacing;
- the complete speech/caption stream once.

During that caption pass, verify several actual word transitions—not just
static frames. The word currently audible must visibly take the accent color,
normally inside a three-to-four-word group, and no group may show more than
four words. Check the opening, densest phrase, shot/speaker changes, and final
caption. A static accent keyword, highlight lag, unreadable accent, or a style
setting that does not produce visible active-word coloring is a blocker.

If a defect is suspected, inspect that local window at frame-level density.
Do not create frame-level evidence for the whole render by default. Contact
sheets should cover regular samples and separate boundary close-ups.

For every speech-emphasis word/name card, check the cue against synchronized
picture and audio in the current render. Confirm that the intended interviewer
or guest says the word as its card first becomes readable, using the final
output timeline and the matching occurrence. Inspect locally at frame-level
density when alignment is uncertain. A perceptibly early or late card,
especially one appearing seconds after the word, blocks approval until fixed.
A contact sheet or transcript timestamp alone cannot establish audio sync.
Apply this check again to affected cues after trims, inserts, or speed changes;
standalone labels in the intentionally silent montage retain editorial timing.

Run deterministic media probes for duration, dimensions, stream presence,
decode errors, black frames, long silence, and suspicious frozen regions. A
detector finding is a lead; inspect the cited interval before deciding.

Compare detections with the assigned intentional silence spans and authored
card/still holds. Verify that planned silence is actually silent, including
all inserted-clip audio; do not exempt unexpected silence elsewhere. A planned
still card is not frozen corruption and a designed dark backdrop is not a
missing picture. Document exact intervals and reasons instead of disabling
audio, freeze, or black-frame checks globally.

## Scorecard

Score only after all blockers are resolved:

- story coherence and meaning: 25;
- hook, momentum, and payoff: 20;
- taste/style execution: 20;
- visual choice and continuity: 15;
- captions and typography: 10;
- technical integrity: 10.

Pass requires at least 90/100 and no blocker. Record category scores and
timestamped notes in `coordinator-qc.json`.

Blockers include clipped or invented speech, missing context that changes
meaning, absent payoff, irrelevant or misleading B-roll, unwanted third-party watermarks/embedded
text, wrong-speaker leaks, unreadable or incorrect captions, face/caption
collisions, accidental black/frozen/silent sections, stale renders, or a final
tail without intentional content.
Repeated B-roll within one short, missing active-word caption coloring, more
than four simultaneously visible caption words, or Style 2 exceeding its
15-second montage or 25-second editorial limits are also blockers.
For the current brief, required Valmera branding is exempt from those asset
and tail exclusions; missing, obscured, or truncated Valmera branding still
needs repair. An authorized native placement adjustment is not brand removal.

## Independent review

The editor's evidence makes review efficient but does not decide the verdict.
The coordinator must inspect the pixels/story itself. It may reuse the exact
preview, transcript, contact sheets, and deterministic probe output; duplicating
downloads and full ASR is unnecessary when checksums match.

Before `ready`, query the live child once and confirm the current EDL version
equals the candidate version and no mutation/render job remains active. This
reconciliation uses a free Valmera lane.

## Repairs and exceptions

Return one consolidated repair packet with every issue. Avoid serial nitpicks
that trigger a render for each observation.

- Editor repair round 1: structural and blocker fixes.
- Editor repair round 2: remaining verified defects.
- Coordinator rescue: one bounded direct pass when the fix is clear.
- Otherwise: `needs_user_review` with preview path, timestamps, failed rubric
  items, and the exact decision or manual action required.

Tool/infrastructure failures become `failed_technical` only after reconciling
job state and one safe retry where the operation is idempotent. Never repeat an
additive mutation merely because its reply was lost.

No status wording may imply approval before this contract passes against the
current preview and live EDL.
