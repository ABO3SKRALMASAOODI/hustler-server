## What they share

- **A hook in the first second that is a contradiction or a stake**, never a greeting: a known name plus something that should not be true.
- **One handle object** (a cereal box, a photo, a launch, a statue) that opens and closes the film.
- **A turn in the middle** that is human (betrayal, doubt, humiliation), then a payoff with a figure.
- **Every figure is exact and checkable**; the close reframes the hook in one sentence.
- **Pictures of the named things themselves**, each on the word that names it; designed cards (a date, a logo duel, a number) where no photo exists.
- **Captions are small and steady**; display type is rare and lands on the verdict word.
- **A voice, not music, carries it.** Valmera never adds music; the films stand on the narrator and a few placed sounds.
