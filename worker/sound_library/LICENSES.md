## Recordings that joined by reference match (owner decision 13 Nov 2026)

The owner's decision of 13 Nov 2026: the reference films judge the sound effects, with no owner audition. Each
recording below joined because its signal (length, attack, tail, spectrum, noise against tone, sweep, level) lies
within the measured limit of a class of sound the reference films place (`reference_moments.json`;
`sound_approval.approve_by_reference`). Its manifest row carries "approved by reference match, owner decision 13 Nov
2026" with the reference moments it matched (film and second).

Source: the Sonniss #GameAudioGDC bundles (2015-2026), professional recordings given away each year by Sonniss and
the recordists named below, fetched from the Internet Archive copies of the bundles (lossless FLAC). Licence: the
Sonniss #GameAudioGDC Bundle Licensing Agreement shipped inside each bundle, kept here as
`licences/sonniss_gameaudiogdc_bundle_licence.txt` (sha256 c381327b..., bundles 2015-2018, 2023, 2026) and
`licences/sonniss_gameaudiogdc_bundle_licence_2019_2020_2024.pdf` (sha256 8a26e011..., as those bundles shipped it).
It grants a worldwide, royalty-free licence to use and modify the sounds in personal and commercial projects without
attribution, and to synchronise them with audio and visual projects (games, films, television, interactive). It
forbids selling the sounds as they come and using them to train artificial-intelligence technology: Valmera does
neither. The recordings are only ever mixed into rendered films; matching them against the references is a
measurement of their signal, not training.

Each file is one sound cut from its source file (`cut_s` in the manifest, seconds), with two short fades and its peak
brought to -3 dBFS; nothing else was done to it. The manifest row keeps the source file's name and sha256, the
bundle, and the hash of the licence that bundle shipped.
